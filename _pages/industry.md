## RAGz

A visual research workspace for public neuroscience clinical trial protocols. Drag and combine topics, questions, and study filters on an interactive canvas, then explore connected source records and cited explanations.

## Moleculizer

A molecular evidence explorer combining chemical similarity and semantic search across ChEMBL receptor-binding data, Tox21 screening labels, and ESOL solubility measurements. Source records and optional cited summaries keep experimental evidence distinct from predictions.

![Moleculizer molecular evidence explorer with dataset and search controls]({{ '/assets/img/projects/moleculizer.png' | relative_url }})

[Read about the project]({{ '/projects/moleculizer/' | relative_url }}) · [View code on GitHub](https://github.com/aralcimcim/moleculizer)
