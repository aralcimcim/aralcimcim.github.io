ONGOING RESEARCH · 2026–2027 · JKU LINZ

Reasoning-Guided Vision-Language Models for WSI Report Generation

This project develops a machine learning framework that analyzes pathology whole-slide images (WSIs) to generate structured medical reports. The model aims to make its reasoning explicit and link report statements to supporting visual evidence on the slide. The goal is to support pathologists with accurate, interpretable reports whose conclusions can be checked against the original images.

Whole-slide images→Reasoning & visual evidence→Structured reports

FEATURED RESEARCH

Chemprop-conditioned diffusion

Molecular property prediction with score-based diffusion conditioned on Chemprop graph representations. My research code investigates toxicity classification on Tox21 and compares diffusion predictions with a Chemprop baseline.
