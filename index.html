About

at the intersection of foundation models and medical data

My work combines applied machine learning, mathematics, and software engineering. I study how graph representations and generative models can support scientific prediction.

My background includes multivariate calculus, numerical optimization, software development with a specialization on machine learning in Life Sciences; in particular, geometric deep learning and diffusion models trained on large-scale molecular datasets.
